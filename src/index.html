Our Painting Services

Service 01

Exterior House Painting

Your home's exterior faces everything the Irish weather throws at it — and in Kilkenny, that means damp, wind, and UV in equal measure. A quality exterior paint job isn't just cosmetic. It seals your walls against moisture penetration, prevents cracking render, and protects your biggest investment.

We use premium masonry paints with proven weather resistance, properly prepare all surfaces, and apply the right number of coats for a finish that lasts. No cutting corners, no rushing.

Whether your home is pebble-dashed, rendered, or exposed stone, we've done it before and we know what works in this county's climate.

Service 03

Roof Painting

Your roof is the most exposed part of your home. Algae, lichen, moss, and years of rain leave concrete tiles porous, stained, and vulnerable. A specialist roof coating seals the surface, restores the colour, and dramatically extends the life of the roof — often for a fraction of what replacement would cost.

We pressure-wash, treat, and coat every roof to a professional standard. The results are immediate: a clean, uniform finish that improves kerb appeal and gives the roof a new lease of life.

Not sure if your roof needs painting or replacement? We'll give you an honest assessment at the free quote stage. No upselling, just straight advice.

Pricing Guidance

What Does House Painting Cost in Kilkenny?

Every job is different, so we always provide a free tailored quote. That said, here's an honest guide to the kind of ranges you can expect — so you can plan with confidence.

Exterior House Painting

€1,500 – €6,000+

Driven by property size, storey count, surface condition, number of coats, and access requirements (scaffolding, ladders). Thorough surface prep is included — never skipped.

Interior Painting

€300 – €3,500+

Ranges from a single room refresh to a full house interior. Ceiling height, surface condition, number of colours, and finish type all affect the final figure.

Roof Painting

€1200 – €3,500+

Dependent on roof size, pitch, tile condition, and the level of cleaning and treatment required before coating. A cost-effective way to extend your roof's lifespan significantly.

All prices are indicative guides only. Your written quote will be accurate and itemised, with no hidden costs. VAT applicable where stated.

How much does it cost to paint a house in Kilkenny?

Exterior house painting in Kilkenny typically ranges from €1,500 to €6,000+ depending on the size of the property, the condition of existing paintwork, the type of paint specified, and access requirements. Smaller semi-detached homes cost less; large detached properties with complex roof lines or two storeys cost more. We always provide a full itemised quote before any work begins — no surprises.

We cover Kilkenny city and all surrounding areas including Thomastown, Callan, Castlecomer, Graiguenamanagh, Piltown, Mullinavat, Urlingford, Bennettsbridge, and Stoneyford. If you're in County Kilkenny or just across the border, get in touch and we'll confirm availability.

What type of paint do you use for exterior house painting?

We use high-quality masonry paints and weather-resistant coatings specifically formulated for the Irish climate — damp-resistant, UV-stable, and breathable. Brands and finishes are discussed with you during the quoting process so you get exactly the look and longevity you're after.

How long does exterior house painting take in Kilkenny?

Most exterior house painting jobs in Kilkenny take between 2 and 5 days, depending on property size, the number of coats required, preparation work needed, and weather conditions. We'll give you a realistic timeframe upfront and keep you updated throughout.

Do you do roof painting in Kilkenny?

Yes — roof painting and roof coating is one of our specialist services. We clean, prepare, and apply durable protective coatings to extend the life of your roof and improve kerb appeal. It's a cost-effective alternative to full roof replacement in many cases. Find out more about our roof painting service.

How often should I repaint the exterior of my Kilkenny home?

In Ireland's climate, exterior paintwork typically needs refreshing every 5–10 years, though this depends on the paint quality used, sun and rain exposure, and whether any surface cracks or moisture issues are addressed promptly. A good repaint done properly will last a long time.

Will you move furniture before interior painting?

We always protect your home thoroughly before interior work begins. We use dustsheets and masking tape to cover floors, furniture, and fittings. For large items, we'll work around them or ask you to move them to a safe spot — we'll confirm exactly what's needed during the quote visit.

Do I need to prepare the walls before you arrive?

Not necessarily. Surface preparation is part of our process. We'll wash down, sand, fill cracks and holes, and apply primer where needed before the finish coat goes on. Good prep is what makes a paint job last — we never skip it.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfer and cash. For larger jobs, we typically request a deposit to secure your booking date, with the balance on completion once you're happy with the work.
